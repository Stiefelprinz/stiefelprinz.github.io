Privacy Policy – Engineering Fails Uploader

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Engineering Fails Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel "Engineering Fails Explained". It is used exclusively by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own channel.

Data accessed

The app uses Google OAuth to obtain permission to upload videos (scope youtube.upload) and to read basic information about the owner's own channel (scope youtube.readonly). It does not access data of any other user.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only locally on the owner's own device. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
